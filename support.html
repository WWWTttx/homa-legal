Support

Homa · 2026-09-29

Getting help

Homa stores everything on your device, so most issues can be resolved without contacting us. If something looks wrong, try Settings → Clear All Data to remove local photo entries, restored versions, voice recordings, memory pages, and remaining Page Credits, then start fresh. Credits cannot be restored. This cannot be undone.

In-app purchases

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store. If a purchase did not grant credits, check your Apple receipt in Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → View Account → Purchase History. Page Credits are consumable and are tracked in an on-device ledger; they do not transfer between devices.

Privacy and your data

Homa does not read your advertising identifier, does not track you across apps, and does not send notifications. Read the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Contact

Email support@homa.example for support requests. For privacy-specific requests, see the Privacy Policy contact address.
