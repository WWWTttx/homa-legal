Privacy Policy

Homa · Effective date: 2026-09-29

Homa is built so that your memories stay on your device. This policy explains what Homa does and does not do with your data.

1. Data We Collect — and What We Do Not

Homa does not operate a backend account tied to you and does not upload your photos, notes, voice memories or restored outputs to any Homa server. All of this content is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (Apple SwiftData).

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or attribution SDKs, and we do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

2. On-Device vs Account Storage

Homa has no account and no login. Everything you create is on-device. There is no cloud profile for us to retain. If you remove the app or use Settings → Clear All Data, the local data is removed from the device. Because there is no account, there is no separate "account deletion" step — clearing all data on the device removes everything Homa holds about you.

3. Device Permissions, As Implemented

  • Camera — requested on demand when you tap Scan Photo, to capture a printed photo. Images are processed on device.
  • Photos (read) — requested on demand when you tap Choose Photo, to import an existing photo you select. Homa accesses only the items you choose.
  • Photos (add-only write) — requested on demand when you tap Save Image, to save a restored page into your photo library. Homa requests add-only access and does not read your library.
  • Microphone — requested on demand for Record Memory and audio capture. Audio is stored locally on your device.
  • Speech Recognition — requested on demand (together with the microphone) for Speak Details, to convert your speech to text locally for the matching field. Audio is not sent to Homa servers.

4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

Homa shows the ATT prompt once on your first home screen only because the system requires it. Homa does not read your advertising identifier (IDFA), does not perform cross-app tracking, and does not integrate any advertising or attribution SDK. Whether you allow or deny, Homa's behavior is identical and no tracking occurs.

5. Notifications

Homa shows the Notifications prompt once on your first home screen only because the system requires it. Homa does not register for remote push notifications and does not send you any notifications — local or remote. Allowing or denying the prompt changes nothing in Homa.

6. In-App Purchases and StoreKit

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit 2. Apple is the merchant of record and handles your payment information; Homa does not receive or store your card details. Homa keeps an on-device record of completed transactions and your remaining Page Credit balance so the app can grant and consume credits. These records stay on your device.

7. Third Parties

The only third party involved in Homa's operation is Apple — for the App Store / StoreKit purchases and for the device system frameworks that provide the camera, photos, microphone and speech capabilities you explicitly use. Homa does not share your data with any other party.

8. Sample and Demo Content

To help you learn the app, Homa may include sample or demo content stored locally on your device. This content is also removed when you use Clear All Data.

9. Children

Homa is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Because Homa stores no account data, it does not profile any user.

10. Your Privacy Requests

For any privacy question or request, contact us at legal@homa.example. Since Homa holds no server-side account data about you, the most direct way to remove your data is Settings → Clear All Data on your device.
