Homa · Effective date: 2026-09-29

Homa is a mobile app that helps you restore printed and old photos and keep them as memory albums. It lets you scan a printed photo and straighten and crop it, restore, enhance or colorize it, attach a date, place, back-of-photo note and a voice memory, and compile a group of photos into a restored memory page (an album spread).

1. Acceptance

By downloading or using Homa you agree to these Terms of Use. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. No Account Required

Homa does not require you to create an account, sign up, or log in. All of your photos, notes, voice memories and restored outputs are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (Apple SwiftData). There is no Homa server account associated with you.

3. What We Store

Because Homa has no account and no backend tied to you, we do not store your content on our servers. Everything you add lives on your device until you remove it. You can permanently remove all of it at any time from Settings → Clear All Data, which deletes photo entries, albums, samples and any remaining credits from the device. This action cannot be undone.

4. In-App Purchases

Homa offers optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store (StoreKit 2). Purchases are consumable "Page Credits" used to generate restored memory pages. One credit is consumed each time a memory page is generated. Available packs are fixed-price in USD:

  • $0.99 · $2.99 · $9.99 · $19.99
  • $29.99 · $49.99 · $69.99 · $99.99

Page Credits are consumable. Consumed credits are not restorable through Apple's "Restore Purchases" mechanism. Your remaining balance is tracked in an on-device ledger on this device only and does not transfer between devices or persist after you clear all data. All billing, pricing, refunds and tax are handled by Apple; please review Apple's terms for purchase and refund policies. Homa does not process or store your payment card details.

5. Permissions We Request

Homa requests device permissions only when you use the matching feature, and some only to satisfy system requirements:

  • Camera — used when you tap Scan Photo to photograph a printed photo. Requested on demand.
  • Photos (read) — used when you tap Choose Photo to import an existing photo. Requested on demand.
  • Photos (add-only write) — used when you tap Save Image to save a restored page to your photo library. Requested on demand.
  • Microphone — used for Record Memory and audio capture. Requested on demand.
  • Speech Recognition — used for Speak Details to turn your speech into text. Requested together with the microphone, on demand.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) — shown once on your first home screen. Homa does not read your advertising identifier, does not perform cross-app tracking, and does not integrate any advertising or attribution SDK. The prompt is displayed only because the system requires it.
  • Notifications — shown once on your first home screen. Homa does not register for remote push and does not send you any notifications (local or remote). The prompt is displayed only because the system requires it.
